东坡肘子 Dōng Pō Zhǒu Zi, DongPo's Pork Shoulder (nothing to do with DongPoRou, which is sweet and braised)

this dish is very fatty, but i really like it :D

ingredients
For the Pork shoulder: 
- A whole Pork shoulder, uncut (can get that at Asian supermarket)
- 2-3 knobs x Ginger (smashed with the side of a clever)
- 1 piece x Dried Clementine Peel
- 1 x Star Anis
- Few x Sichuan Pepper Corn
- 1 small piece x Cinnamon bark 


For the sauce:
- 1 small bowl of oil (a lot!)
- Doubanjiang ( like 4-5 solid spoons)
- Ginger (2-3 knobs thinly chopped)
- 2 x Spring Onion ( thinly chopped)
- 2-3 Spoons x chili powder
- 1 spoon x msg or chicken powder
- 1 x spoon x soy sauce
- 1 spoon x black vinegar
- 1 cherry seed and a half worth of Sichuan peppercorn powder
- 1/2 spoon x Sichuan peppercorn oil
- 1 x entire garlic (thinly chopped)
- a fistful of cilantro


1 - Cooking the Pork shoulder:
- In a Instant Pot, add everything from the ingredient lists, cover with water and cook twice for 35 to 45 minutes
* The broth is a bonus, keep it or throw it

2 - All the flavours lays in the sauce:
- in a pan with the oil, heated, cook doubanjiang and chopped ginger until fragrant
- add chili powder until the colors are nice and cook for like 30 second 1 minute, put the mixture in a bowl
- Add the non veggie ingredient in first, we don't want the hot oil to cook it, then add the veggie ingredient in
- Adjust to your taste

3 - Presentation
Take the pork shoulder out and put it in a bowl, add the sauce on top, enjoy with a steamy bowl of rice
